The application dock in dock/ (DockHost.qml and components/Dock.qml,
DockItem.qml, DockContextMenu.qml, DockAppPicker.qml, DockMenuAction.qml)
is ported from hyprland-dock by Nick Friedrich:
https://github.com/nick-friedrich/hyprland-dock — MIT License.

Adapted for this shell: embedded into the running quickshell instance
(DockHost instantiated from shell.qml instead of a standalone
ShellRoot/Overlay), and extended with auto-dodge — the dock hides when a
window on the active workspace overlaps its area, driven by the shell's
event-fed HyprlandData instead of pointer proximity alone.
