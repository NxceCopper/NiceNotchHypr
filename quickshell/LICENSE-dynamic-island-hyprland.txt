Portions of this quickshell configuration are adapted from the project
"dynamic-island-hyprland" by St0rmosu:
https://github.com/St0rmosu/dynamic-island-hyprland

Specifically:
  - BackgroundWindow.qml          (shell-drawn wallpaper background)
  - scripts/scan_wallpapers.py    (wallpaper scan + thumbnail cache)
  - wallpaper apply/backend logic (WallpaperService.qml)
  - wallpaper settings model      (SettingsService.qml / SettingsPanel.qml)
  - LauncherPanel.qml, FavoriteStar.qml, assets/star.svg, assets/star-filled.svg
  - scripts/get-apps.py           (app scanner for the launcher)
  - HyprlandDispatch.qml, HyprlandData.qml
  - WorkspaceOverviewLayer.qml, WorkspaceOverviewScene.qml,
    WorkspaceOverviewWindow.qml
  - WorkspaceIndicator.qml        (workspace capsule inside the notch)
